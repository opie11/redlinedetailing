Questions

The usual ones.

How long does a coating actually last?

Our pro-grade coating is rated five years with basic upkeep. Realistically: three to four years of genuinely easy washing, longer if you park indoors. We'll tell you the honest number, not the brochure one.

Can you fix scratches, or just hide them?

Correction removes paint defects — swirls, oxidation, light scratches — by leveling the clear coat. Deep scratches that catch a fingernail need touch-up first. We measure paint thickness before touching anything.

Do you need my car all day?

Express is about two hours. Full Reset is a drop-off. Obsessive is two days because coatings need to cure in a controlled bay.

What if it rains?

Rain doesn't hurt a cured coating. We do the work indoors, and we'll happily reschedule a correction job if the humidity is fighting us.

Is this a real business?

Not yet — this whole site is a trial run with made-up prices, made-up reviews, and stock photography. The shine, however, is real.
